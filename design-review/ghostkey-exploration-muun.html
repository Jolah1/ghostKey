What to react to in this playground

Things that may feel right: the light surface lifts the mood at the hardest possible moment, the hero graphic carries the emotion so the copy doesn't have to overwork, and Muun's one-action-per-screen rhythm means a grieving person is never asked to decide more than one thing at a time. Things that may feel wrong: light mode breaks from your dark brand everywhere else, the gift emoji may read as too cheerful for a death, and showing the amount this early is a product decision worth debating. Things that may surprise you: splitting one dense screen into three gentle ones can feel faster, not slower, and a personal note from the person who passed may be the single most reassuring element in the whole product. Steal what works, leave the rest.
