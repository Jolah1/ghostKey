## Summary

GhostKey's voice is a real asset. This pass removes the handful of jargon words and idioms that a reader who learned English formally, reading quickly under stress, would stumble on. Each is a direct find and replace with the answer already chosen. Apply across the product.

## Tone notes

- The comparison headline "Nothing else comes close" is louder than the calm voice elsewhere; "How GhostKey compares" reads as more trustworthy on a money product, and letting a competitor hold a genuine check where earned makes GhostKey's own row more believable.
- The "Why Bitcoin" section is correct to exist for the technical reader, but label it ("For the technically curious") so a beginner knows it is safe to skip.
- Standardise punctuation: the recovery file uses em dashes while the rest of the brand voice is cleaner.

## Privacy lines to add

- Note that SMS is the least private heir channel.
- Add a storage and encryption line by the video Record button.
- Recovery kit storage advice should lead with offline options and name email as the least private.
- Offer the offline or watch only path for checking funds, since a public explorer leaks the address.
